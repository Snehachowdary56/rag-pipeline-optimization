# NorthLight CCU-7 Camera Control Unit

Made-up sample manual for a demo. Not a real product.

## Overview

The CCU-7 camera control unit links a studio camera to the control room over a single SMPTE fiber. It carries video, intercom, tally and control. One CCU-7 supports one camera head.

## Iris and Shading

The shading operator adjusts iris, master black and gain from a remote control panel. Gain can be set from minus 6 decibels to plus 18 decibels in 3 decibel steps. Auto iris is disabled by default during live shows.

## Tally

Tally lights tell the camera operator and talent which camera is on air. Red tally means program and green tally means preview. The CCU-7 receives tally from the production switcher over GPI or IP.

## Fiber Link

The SMPTE fiber link can run up to 3 kilometres. The CCU-7 shows optical power on its front panel. If optical power falls below minus 20 dBm a fiber warning appears and the link should be cleaned.

## Intercom

The CCU-7 carries two intercom channels to the camera operator. Channel one is production and channel two is engineering. Program audio can be sent to the operator headset as well.
