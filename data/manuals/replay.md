# NorthLight Instant Replay Server

Made-up sample manual for a demo. Not a real product.

## Overview

The replay server records multiple camera feeds and plays them back for replays and highlights. A standard system records eight channels and plays back two channels at the same time.

## Recording

Recording is continuous on all channels. The server keeps 24 hours of video on its internal storage. When storage is full the oldest material is overwritten unless it has been saved to a clip.

## Clips

Clips are marked with an in point and an out point. Saved clips are protected from overwriting. Clips can be organised into playlists for highlight packages.

## Slow Motion

Playback speed is controlled with the T-bar on the replay controller. Speeds from minus 100 percent to plus 200 percent are supported. High frame rate cameras give smoother slow motion at 3 times speed capture.

## Export

Clips can be exported as files to network storage. Supported export formats are XAVC and ProRes. Export runs in the background and does not stop recording.
